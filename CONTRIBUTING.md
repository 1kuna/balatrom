# Contributing

[← Back to BalatROM](README.md)

Thanks for wanting to help!

**Bug reports are the most useful thing right now.** [Open an issue](https://github.com/1kuna/balatrom/issues) with your OS, what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.

**Doc fixes** are welcome as pull requests.

**Bigger changes:** open an issue first so we can talk it over before you put in the work.

**Please never post** Balatro game files, built ROMs, saves, or anything with your personal info.
